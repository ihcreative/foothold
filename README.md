# Foothold

**A weekly plan for finding customers.**

Foothold picks three ways to meet buyers each week (events, partners, online groups, talks, directories, and reach-outs), tells you what to say, and learns what works.

## Who it is for

Solo business owners who need customers and don't have a ready-made network. The first focus is solo business coaches in the USA.

## How it works

1. **Tell it your week.** Who you help, your city (or online only), and how much time and money you have.
2. **Get three ways to meet buyers.** Each one shows its type, effort, cost, risk, and what to say. A plan never has more than one event.
3. **Add who you talked to.** Foothold drafts the follow-ups and counts conversations and calls toward a goal of five conversations a week.

## Run it

There is no build step and no dependencies. Open `index.html` in any modern browser, or use the GitHub Pages site for this repository. Progress is saved in your browser (local storage) and stays on your device.

## Current build

**Working:** all three screens, form validation, the plan builder (six ways, three per week, shaped by time, budget, and whether you are online only), capture of who you talked to, drafted follow-ups, counters, saved progress, and keyboard and screen reader support.

**Not connected yet:** live event data, accounts and sign-in, email, billing, and a person-checked library of places.
